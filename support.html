常见问题

  • 记录：选择此刻的心情即可保存；标签、文字、语音转文字和照片都是可选项。
  • 数据：记录默认保存在本机。iCloud 同步取决于设备账号和应用内同步设置；可在设置中导出完整备份或清除本机记录。
  • 账号：本版本不开放新账号注册或登录；无需应用账号即可使用核心记录功能。
  • 隐私与条款：见 隐私政策、使用条款。

反馈时请尽量附上：系统版本、App 版本、问题步骤。也可在 App「设置 → 关于 → 联系与反馈」提交。

FAQ

  • Recording: Choose a mood to save a moment. Tags, text, voice dictation, and photos are optional.
  • Data: Records are stored on-device by default. iCloud sync depends on your device account and in-app setting; Settings lets you export a full backup or erase local records.
  • Account: This version does not offer new account registration or sign-in; core recording works without an app account.
  • Privacy & Terms: Privacy Policy, Terms of Use.

When writing, include iOS version, app version, and steps to reproduce. You can also use Settings → About → Contact & feedback in the app.
