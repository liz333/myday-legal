常见问题

  • 计时 / 归档：点开始计时；结束时选择标签保存。不足 1 分钟不会保存。
  • 订购 / 恢复购买：设置 → 我的订购；也可在 iOS「设置 → Apple ID → 订阅」管理自动续订。
  • 数据：默认保存在本机。清空数据或删除账号请见应用内设置。
  • 隐私与条款：见 隐私政策、使用条款。

反馈时请尽量附上：系统版本、App 版本、问题步骤。也可在 App「关于 → Bug 反馈」提交。

FAQ

  • Timer / archive: Tap to start; pick a tag when ending. Sessions under 1 minute are not saved.
  • Subscription / restore: Settings → My subscription; manage auto-renew in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.
  • Data: Stored on-device by default. See in-app Settings to clear data or delete your account.
  • Privacy & Terms: Privacy Policy, Terms of Use.

When writing, include iOS version, app version, and steps to reproduce. You can also use About → Bug feedback in the App.
