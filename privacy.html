隐私政策

生效日期：2026-10-13
开发者：LumDay（developer.lumday@outlook.com）
应用：「一记天光」（LumDay）

一、你保存的信息

你创建的心情片刻、标签、文字、所选照片、每日手写日记和应用偏好保存在设备上的应用数据库或文件中。未完成的记录草稿仅保存在本机，不计入回放分析或手动备份。你可以不登录使用核心记录功能。

二、iCloud 与账号

如果设备已登录 iCloud 且你未选择“仅本地”，本应用会使用 Apple 的 CloudKit 私有数据库同步业务记录。实际同步由 Apple 系统负责，可能需要时间。本版本不开放新的 LumDay 账号注册或登录；既有账号仅保留退出、清理及必要的身份恢复入口，iCloud 同步不依赖该应用账号。Apple 服务受 Apple 自己的隐私政策约束。旧版时间记录的图片文件可能仍只保存在原设备。

三、语音转文字与照片

只有你主动使用语音转文字时，应用才会请求麦克风和语音识别权限。Apple 系统语音服务负责识别；是否联网及音频处理方式可能受系统设置影响。应用不将原始录音作为记录长期保存，也不把音频或转写发送到开发者自有服务器。照片仅在你通过系统照片选择器选中后读取。只有你选择将分享图片保存到照片图库时，应用才会请求添加照片权限。

四、应用锁

你可以设置独立的应用密码并选择启用 Face ID。密码验证信息存储在本应用专用的设备 Keychain 项目中。应用锁用于遮挡界面访问，不加密应用数据库，也不能代替设备密码。

五、手动备份

备份文件包含当前与旧版记录、日记、标签及可用附件。新格式以归档文件逐项处理附件；旧版 JSON 备份的读取上限为 64 MiB。备份未加密，请保存在安全位置。导入前会校验文件，并以合并方式添加本机缺少的数据，不会先清空现有内容。

六、分析与分享

情绪分析依据你明确保存的心情、时间和标签，只描述记录中的共同出现情况，不判断原因、诊断心理状况或提供医疗建议。分享图片由你选择内容并预览后生成；本应用不会将分享内容上传到开发者服务器或创建公开链接。

七、诊断、广告与数据保留

本版本不集成广告追踪 SDK，也不启动产品用量分析服务。若你在系统设置中选择共享崩溃诊断数据，将由 Apple 按其政策处理。数据通常保留到你删除、清除本机数据或管理 iCloud 数据为止；手动备份文件由你自行管理。关闭 iCloud 前，请先确认需要保留的数据已有备份。

八、儿童与联系我们

本应用并非面向 13 周岁以下儿童。若你认为我们误收集了儿童信息，或有隐私及数据请求，请联系 developer.lumday@outlook.com。

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-13
Developer: LumDay (developer.lumday@outlook.com)
App: LumDay (“一记天光”)

1. Information you save

Mood moments, tags, text, selected photos, daily handwritten journals, and app preferences are stored in the app's database or files on your device. An unfinished draft stays on this device and is excluded from replay analysis and manual backups. You can use the core recording features without signing in.

2. iCloud and account sign-in

When your device is signed in to iCloud and Local Only is off, business records use Apple's CloudKit private database. Apple controls synchronization and timing; it may take time. This version does not offer new LumDay account registration or sign-in. Existing accounts retain only sign-out, cleanup, and necessary identity-recovery paths; iCloud sync does not depend on a LumDay account. Apple's services are governed by Apple's privacy terms. Image files attached to legacy time records may remain only on the device where they were created.

3. Dictation and photos

The app requests microphone and speech-recognition access only when you choose dictation. Apple's system speech service performs recognition; network use and audio handling can depend on your system settings. The app does not retain raw audio as a journal entry or send audio or transcripts to a developer-operated server. Photos are read only after you select them with the system photo picker. Add-only Photos access is requested only when you choose to save a shared image to Photos.

4. App lock

You may set an app-specific passcode and choose to enable Face ID. Its verifier is stored in a Keychain item dedicated to this app on this device. The app lock controls interface access; it does not encrypt the app database or replace your device passcode.

5. Manual backups

A backup includes current and legacy records, journals, tags, and available attachments. The current archive format processes attachments as individual entries; reading legacy JSON backups is limited to 64 MiB. Backups are unencrypted, so keep the file somewhere secure. Imports are validated and merged with missing data; they do not clear existing records first.

6. Analysis and sharing

Emotion analysis uses moods, times, and tags that you explicitly saved. It describes co-occurrence in your records; it does not identify causes, diagnose a mental-health condition, or provide medical advice. You choose and preview content before a share image is created. The app does not upload that content to a developer-operated server or create a public link.

7. Diagnostics, advertising, and retention

This version does not integrate an ad-tracking SDK or start a product-usage analytics service. Crash diagnostics you choose to share in system settings are handled by Apple under Apple's terms. Data generally remains until you delete it, clear local data, or manage iCloud data. You manage manual backup files. Before turning off iCloud, make sure you have backed up data you want to keep.

8. Children and contact

The app is not directed to children under 13. For privacy questions or data requests, email developer.lumday@outlook.com.
